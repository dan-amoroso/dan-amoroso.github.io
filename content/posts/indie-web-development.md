The videogame industry is going through an interesting time. After the indie
game golden age in the 2010s, videogame production has been industrialised.
Suits saw the potential margins. Triple A videogames had existed previously:
high budget productions that arguably pushed the limits of what could be done.
But we are now seeing the effects that Suits tend to have in the long run.

Enshittification: Whatever does not effect the bottomline, and often much more
than that, has to go. Fat to be trimmed off.

AAA games flopping does not seem like such a weird thing now, the public is
increasingly rebelling to this shi(f)t. I love to see it.

### Can this happen for WWW too?

What would it take for a similar change to happen in the software industry at
large? Saas is closer in nature to the "live services" that the videogame
industry has developed to keep people hooked to a single title for a long(er)
time, an attempt to develop a subscription business model in videogames, which
normally have a relative short 'half-life' where newer titles tend to take over
the userbase of older ones.
